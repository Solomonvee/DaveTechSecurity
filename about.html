About us

Welcome to Dave-Tech — a trusted name in security door solutions, built on experience, professionalism, and customer satisfaction. Founded by a highly skilled Nigerian technician born in Osun State, Dave-Tech has grown into a reliable brand known for delivering quality door sales, maintenance, installations, and repair services across different categories of security doors. With a strong educational and technical background, the founder holds qualifications including a Higher National Diploma (HND), National Diploma (ND), Diploma in Computer Engineering (2011), and certifications in Information, Communication & Technology. In addition, professional proficiency certifications in Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE 1, 2 & 3) were obtained in 2024, reflecting a commitment to safe and professional service delivery. Originally trained as a Mechanical Engineering graduate with specialization in plant installation and maintenance, he gained over five years of hands-on industrial experience in machine servicing, motor part overhauls, and equipment repairs. This technical foundation later evolved into a full-time career in the security door industry. Today, with more than 15 years of experience as a seasoned door technician, Dave-Tech specializes in the sales, maintenance, and repair of: Local Nigerian security doors Israeli security doors Turkish security doors American security doors Our expertise, attention to detail, and deep understanding of door systems have earned us the trust of homeowners, businesses, and organizations seeking dependable security solutions. At Dave-Tech, our watchwords are: Affordability. Accountability. Reliability. Availability. These values drive everything we do — from customer service to installation and after-service support. We believe security should not only be strong and durable, but also accessible and professionally managed. Whether you need a new security door, expert maintenance, or urgent repairs, Dave-Tech remains committed to providing lasting solutions designed to protect what matters most.
